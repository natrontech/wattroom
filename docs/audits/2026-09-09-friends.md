## Checked and found sound

- **The rider page's audience.** `SharesRoomOrFriends` gates the page; a room-mate gets presence only for rooms they are in (`WhereIs` answers nothing for a room the viewer is not a member of, tested: "in a room alice is not in: a room-mate learns nothing at all"); friends alone get shared rides and the month.
- **A ban cuts both ways.** `TestTrophyCaseVisibilityAfterBan` covers the banned rider reading the case and the members he left behind reading him; friendship outlives the ban on purpose.
- **A non-self viewer's case** strips XP progress and the counts (voice minutes, coached sessions, tracks played); only the medal tally leaked, and now only from rooms in common.
- **Presence on the friends list** is one batched `WhereIs` per request, not one per friend (`TestFriendsPanelBatchesRoomLookups`).
- **A dismissal tells the requester once** and a new ask or an acceptance clears the tombstone (`TestADismissalTellsTheRequester`); the undo now clears it too.
- **Friend codes** are eight characters from an unambiguous alphabet, unique in the table, and the door answers the same 404 to an unknown code and a banned pair.
- **No display name reaches a log line** in either package; errors go out through `httpx.WriteError` with curated text.
