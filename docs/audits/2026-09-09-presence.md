## Checked and found sound

- **The room boundary holds on the wire**: presence is attached only inside the member loop; a crew room you are not in carries name, icon, crew and access and nothing else, and no slug you may not enter.
- **Crew counts cannot leak**: the switcher sums riding/voice/unread over member rooms only; the crew page fetches no presence.
- **A friend's room is named only to its members** (ADR-0012), on the friends list and the rider page alike.
- **Presence folds across a rider's sockets**: two tabs are one person; one closing does not go offline; the per-rider socket cap is refcounted.
- **Unread is server truth**: your own lines never count, `room_reads` is honoured, a rider standing in the room is suppressed by id.
- **One arrival, one announcement**: `(tag, at)` dedup through localStorage; the in-room and rail paths share a tag.
- **ADR-0042's "not looking" rule everywhere**: hidden or unfocused gets the OS notification, never a focused window; permission is asked from a click on the notifications page, never on load.
- **Cues respect the mixer**; the chat-line ping is per line, never per tick; the away and voice cue effects already guarded a reconnect burst.
