## Checked and found sound

- **The queue's ceilings** — 50 entries, 200 queued tracks, 50 per playlist, per ADR-0026 — each refused with a rider-readable reason that reaches the panel.
- **The playhead chase** implements SPEC's numbers exactly (1.5 s seek, 0.25 s deadband, ±5 % rate, 1.2 s settle) on server time; livestreams ride the edge; the paused scrub re-seeks.
- **`ended` is deduplicated** on video, track and anchor, so N reports advance once; both decks report the end from the shared playhead, so a deck playing to an empty room cannot run off.
- **RMF elsewhere holds** — fullscreen pauses this client rather than playing off-screen; a modal or the People sheet un-seats the dock rather than covering it; pasted playlists are cued, never loaded; captions are unloaded because chrome is hidden; the side panel's seat is 208 px at its 240 px minimum.
- **Undo over confirm** for remove and skip-playlist, ten seconds server-side, the toast carrying restore.
- **Permissions** match SPEC's matrix: deck verbs member-level and throttled at 300 ms, board fires at 1 s, playlist rename/delete/remove-track and autoplay moderator-only, the client hiding what it may not do.
- **Library scope** — playing through `visible_rooms` with the crew ban honoured and a 404 rather than a 403; browsing and editing uploader-only; the crew-ban door tested.
- **The duck's numbers** — 25 %, 150 / 600 / 400 ms — in one state machine in one home.
- **Vocabulary** — no rider-facing "pool" or "shelf"; `/music` is in the phone sweep; the smart shuffle's SQL is SPEC's four factors in one pass, room- and member-scoped.
