## Checked and found sound

- **AV is never recorded** — no `MediaRecorder`, egress or `roomRecord` grant anywhere; the token grant is join, publish, subscribe only, scoped to the canonical slug with a `riderID#nonce` identity and a 30-minute TTL; 401 and 403 split as errors.md asks; webhooks verify HMAC, body hash, issuer and expiry before being believed.
- **SPEC's audio numbers are honoured, not invented** — the mic constraints (NS off, EC on, AGC on), music-quality opus without DTX, a 48 kHz transmit context, the gate's threshold, hysteresis, hold and attack/release, the doubling under music, the speaking hold, the duck's level and timings — each in one file.
- **The gate rides gain, never the track's mute**, and mute is unpublish, so the mic chip reflects only the rider's own toggle.
- **A full reconnect keeps the gated track** (LiveKit skips `restartTrack` for user-provided tracks) and mute state, which is our own `micOn`.
- **A device unplugged mid-voice** raises a persistent fault, closes the chain and goes muted, drawn as a fault banner with one big button.
- **A hidden tab** keeps its level meter on an AudioWorklet, and both audio graphs resume on `visibilitychange`.
- **A stuck push-to-talk key** cannot happen: `keyup`, `blur` and `visibilitychange` all release, and Space yields to text fields and focused controls.
- **The sink pick on Firefox and Safari** hides rather than fails, on every picker.
- **The duck un-ducks** — one module-scope machine, the release timer outside any effect cleanup, `talk.clear()` on disconnect, a regression test.
- **Screen-share audio** is its own track on its own fader, keyed so it cannot evict the rider's voice, captured with processing off per ADR-0011, ducking under voice while voices never do.
- **Two tabs** — newest claim wins on server time, announced only on an explicit takeover, `micLiveElsewhere` keeps a stand-down from reading as "muted", the rejoin note yields to a mic held elsewhere.
- **One source of truth per flag** — `micOn`, `camOn`, `sharing` live on `AvState` and are cross-checked against the publication; `mode`, `threshold`, `pttHeld` live in the gate settings. The 1222-line module hides no duplicate.
- **The device singleton (#1869)** is read by the room's chain and `/settings/voice` alike, every in-room write re-applies the sink; only the camera write was incomplete (#1876).

## Not checked

Needs a running app, hardware or two riders: whether the reconnect takeover (#1878) fires against a real SFU restart; real sink switching on Chrome, Firefox and Safari, and `setSinkId` on an unplugged device; loopback share audio per platform (`sharingAudio` reading false where the platform refuses); the gate against a real fan at SPEC's thresholds; whether the AudioWorklet blob survives the production CSP (#1737's report-only policy is the instrument); LiveKit's adaptive-stream and dynacast layers on a real uplink; the webhook → hub voice loop end to end; XP for lounge presence; and how the mix sounds, which no test can assert.
