# Audit: security and privacy since the full review, 2026-10-25

**The slice.** Security and privacy of what merged into `server/` and `web/src` between the [2026-09-25 full review](2026-09-25-full-review.md) (`ab669885`) and `75a45be23`: 450 commits, most of them Ride Worlds. Five areas, in this order: the HTTP endpoints and WS commands added since; route privacy under [ADR-0063](../decisions/0063-a-route-keeps-its-place-with-care.md); the phone remote's relay (#4025); uploads and parsers; the two open SvelteKit Dependabot alerts. Supply chain got a short check at the end.

**Excluded**: UX, density, performance and code style. Real hardware. Production and `janlauber/homelab`. Route rows, GPX files, coordinates and Strava payloads, none of which were read (AGENTS.md hard rules). Code and the synthetic fixtures were.

**Method.** Read-only. Five reviewers took one area each, from a shared brief: the non-route endpoints, the routes package, route data leaving its audience, the new WS commands, uploads and parsers. The orchestrator read the remote relay and the SvelteKit paths itself. It then re-read the code behind every high and medium finding and every finding it fixed. New endpoints were found by diffing every `HandleFunc` pattern against `ab669885` (26 new), and new WS commands by diffing `protocol.ClientMessage` (Roadside, Drive, Remote, Gear and Pause). Nothing was reproduced in a running app. The fixes below were each seen red in a test first.

## A question for the maintainer

**How far from a route's ends does a crew's line start?** `protocol.RouteHiddenEndM` is 400 (`protocol/limits.go:173`), so the crew's polyline and road start 400 m from the true ends (`routes/road.go:119`, `:217`). Every other private-end rule is 1,000 m: corridor tiles, efforts near an end, and the segment private distance (`docs/spec/roads.md:55-57`, `:68`). ADR-0063 says both "400 m at both ends" and "tiles withheld within max(anchor distance, 1,000 m)". A line that reaches 400 m makes the tiles' 1,000 m moot. Mid-route zones (#3132) are not built, so a route that passes home in its middle hides nothing there yet.

- **Raise it to 1,000 m now** (recommended): one constant and the tests that assume 400. It matches every other distance, and ADR-0063 cites a study that recovered homes from a few routes. Cost: a route under about 2.1 km has no crew span.
- **Keep 400 m until the geo pack's anchors land.** That is the reading the code follows today, and it holds only if the anchors come soon.
- **Do nothing.** Then amend the spec so the 1,000 m tile rule says it applies to tiles only.

Tightening needs no ADR to stay inside the rules, but the 400 m is ADR-0063's own number, so either change amends it.

## The two SvelteKit alerts

Both were dismissed as `not_used`.

- **#8, an open redirect through `use:enhance`.** WattRoom has no form actions: no `use:enhance`, no `$app/forms` import and no `+page.server.ts` in `web/src`. The build is `adapter-static` with an SPA fallback (`web/vite.config.ts:104`), so no route takes a `//` action path.
- **#7, XSS through a prerendered page's `cache-control`.** No page calls `setHeaders`, and `cache-control` appears nowhere in `web/src`. The `(site)` pages prerender (`web/src/routes/(site)/+layout.ts`), and their headers come from the Go server, never from a load function.

The high happy-dom alert closed with #4131. No Dependabot alert is open.

## Sound

What was checked and holds, so the next audit need not re-derive it.

**The phone remote's relay (#4025).** A remote press reaches only the sender's own sockets: `remoteHolders` reads the trainer claim keyed by the sender's rider id and returns only that rider's sockets on the holding tab, never the sender's own tab (`hub/remote.go:47-74`). Gear and pause go the other way under the same key, and only from the holder (`remote.go:118-136`). The action is from a closed set before it reaches the rate-limit map (`protocol/remote.go:24-39`). The gear label is bounded to 16 runes. A tab label is clipped to 64 characters and only ever compared within one rider (`hub/pairing.go:58`). The phone uses the same `/ws/channels/{id}` door, crew membership included, and counts against the 16-socket cap (`hub/hub_sockets.go:8`). Nobody can command another rider's tab.

**Endpoints.**

- Blocks (#3368): sign-in first, UUID parsed, self-hide refused, 404 for an unknown or not-hidden rider, list and delete scoped to `blocker_id` (`blocks/blocks.go:109-184`, `queries/blocks.sql:13-24`). Every gate #3368 promised is in place: DMs, DM heads, reactions, friends, presence, the rider page, pokes, cheers and open rides.
- Wardrobe: buy checks the item, its kind, ownership and the balance under the rider's row lock (`wardrobe/service.go:87-109`). `Spend` and `Refund` fail on a reused ref (`wallet/wallet.go:127-144`). An outfit checks every slot against the caller's own wardrobe under the lock (`wardrobe/outfit.go:45-108`). Look values are bounded, and colours map through a palette (`web/src/lib/world/outfit.ts:126-127`).
- `GET /api/looks/{hash}`: sign-in, hash shape, nosniff. The look hash stays off open rides and reaches only the voice roster behind channel admission.
- The figure: a closed set, bounded again in the database (`20261014233651_user_figure.sql`), sent only to `/api/me` and the export.
- The wallet: minting is bounded per ride (1.2 × minutes pedalled), per UTC save day (180, under the row lock in both savers) and unique per `(user, source, ref)`. No source transfers between accounts.
- intervals.icu: 32 random bytes of state in an HttpOnly, Lax cookie, compared in constant time; fixed redirect targets; a 20 s timeout and a 16 MiB body cap; at most 50 workouts of 1 MiB; a pull id that is random, owner-checked and single-use. The token is never stored.
- The Strava webhook: mounted only with a verify token, compared in constant time; 16 KiB body bound; logs carry the user id only.
- Every audited mutating route refuses a cross-origin request (`auth/session.go:72-76`), answers in errors.md's one shape, and never puts `err.Error()` in a message.

**Routes (ADR-0063).**

- Every routes endpoint takes the session only, never `readAuth`, so a personal token reads no route (`server/wire.go:207-211`).
- List, get, rename and delete are owner-scoped in SQL, so another rider's route is a 404 (`queries/routes.sql:32-52`).
- `/road` checks the hash before anything else. A crew gets the cut with heights from zero (`road/cut.go:25`), and a Strava route gets 403.
- `Attach` decides the cut by the route's real owner, so a caller passing the wrong viewer cannot leak the whole road (`routes/attach.go:168`).
- Plans and picks refuse another rider's route and every Strava route (`attach.go:55-75`, `:197-226`), games and races included.
- The ghost and attempts read the caller's own rides only, and the ghost carries metres, never coordinates (`attempts_test.go:72-106`). Other riders' ghosts, `board_ok` and segment efforts are not built yet.
- Create: a 5 MB body cap; unknown fields refused; 2–200 km; a polyline of at most 50,000 points, all on the globe; at most 32 climbs; a generated name built from numbers; 10 a minute and 200 in all, counted under the row lock.
- With no key, the shape is dropped and the stored road is bare. Reseal is compare-and-swap.
- World secrets are domain-separated HMACs (`road/worldkey.go:20-36`), and region secrets go to the owner only (`routes/world_test.go:150`).
- Delete cascades consents, nulls the plans' and rides' `route_id`, and `Attach` then answers "deleted". Purge removes routes through the owner cascade.

**ADR-0063's "every place a name travels"**, one verdict each:

- The session state sent to the channel: the name is rewritten (`hub/roads.go:47-65`), the tick zeroes `m` and `alt` (`hub/channel_ride.go:44-48`), and `SessionRoute` carries only the generated name.
- The presence radar reads the shared name (`hub/presence.go:83`, `:131`).
- Email subjects and leads read the plan row stored under the generated name (`crews/plan_road.go:43`, `notify/session_mail.go:187`). No test: #4154.
- ICS reads the same (`crews/calendar.go:136`, `:167`), tested for the generated name only.
- The Strava title reads `rides.workout_name`, and the `.fit` carries no position (`fitexport/fitexport.go:92-93`). No test: #4154.
- Friends' shared rides carry `workout_name` and numbers only (`queries/riders.sql:120-136`).
- The og title and the card's filename: og serves crews only (`og/og.go:107-110`), and the ride card uses the generated name with no height labels (`rides/card.go:83-112`).
- The recap is written from the shared name (`hub/recap.go:95`).
- MCP reads a road ride as "Route ride" plus km and gain (`mcp/mcp.go:286-295`).

A co-rider's Strava upload carries distance and relative heights of the coach's cut (`hub/world_record.go:34-43`, `strava/upload.go:73-79`) but no position. ADR-0063 already answers this: "Other riders keep the metres and relative heights they rode: their ride is theirs."

**WS commands.**

- Every new kind has its own allowance (`roadside` 250 ms, `control:` and `remote:` per action) or is a single write to the sender's own socket (`Drive`, `Device`, `Away`).
- Roadside: closed sets for verb and stamp; NaN, Inf and a negative lap refused; a session rider refused; stands 300–5,000 m ahead, moved once per 60 s; paint capped at 6, 12 and 24; refusal codes from the closed set (`hub/roadside.go:43-78`).
- `Drive` sets only the sender's `ergByRoad`, which can only untime the sender (`hub/pairing.go:132-136`).
- The control vocabulary is unchanged since `ab669885` (`protocol/control.go:33-40`). Race, Last Light and Wheelrace go through the coach-only branch, and their minutes are validated (`ws_messages.go:184-191`).
- Race positions come from watts on the server, with the weight frozen at the flag and an interpolated photo finish (`race/race.go:87-124`). The client's `M` is never read. Race saves are restamped from the race trail, and bunch rides are towed and untimeable.
- `M` and `Alt` are bounded to 0–200 km and −500…9,000 m, and the tick zeroes both.
- Open rides are not built (#3304 closed), and their wire types cannot carry a name, a number or a look (`TestAnOpenRideCarriesNothingAboutAnyone`).

**Uploads and parsers.**

- Every request body is bounded. The 56 `httpx.DecodeStrict` callers cap at 64 KiB. The bespoke readers use `MaxBytesReader`: rides 4 MB, routes 5 MB, fitexport 4 MB, feedback 512 KiB, board 4 MB, tracks 48 MB. There is no multipart and no `ParseForm`. The WS read limit is 512 KiB (`hub/ws.go:42`).
- `road.UnpackRoad` caps samples at length/10 and requires 2–200 km and an exact byte count. `DecodePolyline6` checks the alphabet, the varint width, 50,000 points and the globe.
- `workout.Validate` bounds steps, repeats (50), depth (4), segments (200) and a day's length. The hub pick runs `Validate` and `CheckLength`.
- The web's workout import checks 1 MB before reading and drops `.zwo` text events. The route import checks 5 MB before reading. `fit.ts` bounds every read by the header's end, and a zero-size record still advances.
- A ride upload bounds its sample count to 6 h, every field, `m` and `alt`, and 30 m/s between samples. Its distance comes from the server's replay. The daily XP and wallet caps hold under `LockUser`.
- Track uploads (#2925) still take one in flight per rider, a 10-minute read deadline, a streamed 48 MB cap and the 2 GB quota under the lock. #4148 is the gap left.
- The web has one `{@html}`, on escaped JSON-LD (`site/seo.ts:203`), and no `innerHTML`.

**Not checked**: whether a ride upload can carry an embedded road copy inside `workoutJson`. Only `workout.Parse` runs there, not `RoadOf`.
