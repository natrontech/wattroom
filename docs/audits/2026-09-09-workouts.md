## Checked and found sound

- **Zones** match SPEC's Coggan table exactly; `plannedZoneSeconds` walks ramps per second.
- **The graph** is `viewBox` + `w-full` with no fixed width; glow only on the elapsed cursor (ADR-0005).
- **The client validator** bounds name, seconds, fraction, watts, cadence, HR, repeat depth, the expanded count and the total — the two whole-workout caps most implementations forget.
- **Ownership**: `owner_id` in every WHERE; 404 for someone else's id, tested on update and delete.
- **Editor history**: 100-snapshot cap, 600 ms coalescing, redo truncation, reset on hydration; ⌘Z defers to the browser inside fields.
- **Tree operations at depth** and the **engine's** nested repeats, ramp interpolation, watts override, null target on sprint — all tested.
- **Four states** on both surfaces, a `dropped` count for unreadable rows; **stacking order** steps → step → library on a phone; the shared workout is validated before `flatten`.

#1714 (sharing a custom workout beyond a coach's pick); the per-rider ceiling stays #1414's; whether a mid-workout ramp is scored (#1709).
