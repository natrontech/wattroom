## Checked and found sound

- **One permission expression, both bans, exclusions after the union.** `visible_rooms` (migration `20260908145114:131-170`, rewritten by #1236 in `20260908204419:36-62`); `CanEnterRoom`/`IsBannedFromRoom` are the only doors (`queries/crews.sql:83-106`), exercised by `store/visible_rooms_test.go` and `store/crew_invariants_test.go`.
- **Non-members get 404, not 403** (`crews.go:131-162`, tested): a crew's existence is not public the way a slug is.
- **The owner is un-demotable and un-bannable, and cannot leave** (`crew_write.go:99-108`, `crew_join.go:128-131`, `crew_ownership.go:51-56`, migration `20260908191303`).
- **A banned rider cannot re-enter via the code, and is told at the door** (`crew_join.go:46-58,101-104`, `crew_ban_test.go`).
- **A crew role is not a door**: a role for a stranger's id is refused (`crew_write.go:115-123`); a grant to a non-crew-mate is refused (`grants.go:57-66`).
- **A room a caller may not enter carries no slug on the wire** (`crew_access.go:34-46`, tested).
- **The purge transfers before it deletes**, and `on delete restrict` makes forgetting loud (`account.go:360-376`, `crew_ownership.go:63-74`).
- **Logging** carries UUIDs and slugs only: no code, no name, no email.
- **401 across the whole crew surface** (`boundary_audit_test.go`), four states on the crew page, its settings and the door (retry withheld on `not_found`), an empty state that teaches on the crew's room list, context menus on the crew row, a person and a room, phone width covered by `e2e/phone-width.spec.ts`, six-character codes from SPEC's alphabet, engineering bounds on every crew list.

## Decisions surfaced

- **Undo or confirm on a crew ban.** Landed as a confirm (ADR-0038 calls a crew ban imperative); a reversible ban that keeps memberships hidden is a second state on `memberships` and was not taken.
- **Should a private-room grant survive leaving the crew?** Landed as no — the leave dialog already promised it. The alternative (a grant as a standing personal door) would have to be said at the door and be revocable from a screen the grantee can see.
- **Should a room owner be able to leave someone else's crew?** Kept as is (a room never moves, ADR-0038); the copy now names deleting the room as the other way out.
