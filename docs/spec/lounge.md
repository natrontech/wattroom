# The cozy room

A voice channel between sessions is its room (`WATTROOM.md`), until the rider picks Tiles, which this device remembers. A camera takes a strip along its bottom, and a shared screen or a video hangs on its wall. Everything in it is paint: nothing reaches a ride, and no rider's numbers leave one.

## What it remembers

| What                                    | Number                                                |
| --------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------- |
| Lamp, stove, weather, mood, radio, pets | kept per voice channel, written at most every **2 s** |
| Polaroids on the corkboard              | the last **3** sessions                               |
| Notes on the corkboard                  | **6** at most, one a pin                              |
| Medals on the wall                      | **5** at most                                         |
| Plant sizes                             | at **1, 5, 20, 50** sessions ridden together          |
| High scores                             | a crew's best **5**, a rider once each                |

## Riders in it

| What                        | Number                                                                                 |
| --------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Places                      | the sofa's **4**, then desk, stove, two beanbags, two stools, arcade, workshop: **12** |
| Places only a tall room has | the last **6**                                                                         |
| One idle thing              | **50 s**, nothing at all about 3 times in 10                                           |
| An emote shows for          | **6 s**                                                                                |
| A towel after a ride        | **10 min**                                                                             |
| Bulbs a rider lights        | every 3rd, every 2nd at two riders, all at three                                       |

## A picture on the wall

| What                 | Number                                                                  |
| -------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| The room's pixel     | one whole step smaller, so the floor stays and the wall grows           |
| The screen           | **16:9**, centred, from **10** rows under the ceiling to the sofa's top |
| Hangs above the room | where the room's pixel would fit under **3**: a phone                   |
| A ride's Room view   | the same room, under the ride's numbers                                 |

## Visitors

**12** visitors, each with its own weather. One slot in two of **5 min** brings one, for **24 s**. The album keeps the first time a crew saw each, and never who saw it.

## Furnishing

A member pays from their own Batzen, and it goes up for the whole crew. What the crew owns is free to swap.

## Bergpreis

The arcade's game: **30 s** on one button. Ten points a metre, **9'999** the most the machine shows. A run with the needle always in the band scores well under that.

## The calendar

Fasnacht runs from Schmutziger Donnerstag to the Tuesday, 52 to 47 days before Easter. Easter runs Good Friday to Easter Monday. New Year and 1 August start at 18:00 the evening before, and Samichlaus the same on 5 December. The yellow jersey hangs 1 to 27 July, blossom falls 1 April to 15 May, leaves 20 September to 15 November. Fondue replaces Friday's pizza from December to February.
