# The cozy room

A voice channel between sessions is its room (`WATTROOM.md`), until the rider picks Tiles, which this device remembers. A camera takes a strip along its bottom, and a shared screen or a video hangs on its wall. Everything in it is paint: nothing reaches a ride, and no rider's numbers leave one.

## The arcade

The cabinet stands in a tall room's front row, and the room's bar opens it in any room. It holds three games, each on one button and a **160 × 90** screen. The device remembers the last one picked.

| What          | Number                                                                                                                                      |
| ------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Daily course  | one seed a **UTC** day, the same for every crew: the days since 1970                                                                        |
| A rider's day | their best run at each game that day; a worse one changes nothing                                                                           |
| Boards        | today's best **5** and the all-time best **5**, the first to reach a score winning a tie                                                    |
| Weekly cup    | each day's best run at each game wins that day; the cup counts wins since **Monday**, in UTC like the crew streak                           |
| Record holder | the all-time best at a game: a crown on their room figure, and their letters on the cabinet's screen in turn with the score, **2.5 s** each |
| Your best     | your all-time best at each game; beating it says **New best** with a fanfare                                                                |

A score is paint, like everything in the room: the client's word is enough, and it pays no XP and no Batzen.

### Bergpreis

**30 s** up a hill. Ten points a metre, **9'999** the most the machine shows. A run with the needle always in the band scores well under that. The seed sets the two waves the band drifts on.

### Feed zone

**30 s** past the roadside, the road going from **8** to **15 m/s**. Someone stands every **12** to **30 m**: a bidon (**50** points, within **1.3 m**) about half the time, a musette (**120**, within **0.8 m**) one in four, a fan with a flag one in five. A tap reaches out for **0.28 s**, and the arm is back after **0.42 s**. Every three grabs in a row add one to the multiplier, up to **×4**. A grab at nothing, a soigneur passed by or a flag grabbed breaks the streak. **9'999** the most the machine shows.

### Darts

Three darts at a dartboard on the corkboard, the standard board's numbers from 20 at the top. The aim sways **24 px**; holding the button steadies it by up to **75 %** over **0.9 s**, and past **1.8 s** the hand starts to shake. Letting go throws. Doubles on the outer ring, trebles on the inner one, **25** and **50** for the bull: **180** at most. A dart throws itself after **7 s**. The seed sets the sway and a draught of up to **3 px**.
