- On a road with a classified climb, riding alone or in a session, your bike computer turns to a CLIMB page by itself as the climb begins, with a short sound. It shows which climb of how many and its class, how far and how much height to the top, and the average gradient left, above a profile of the climb with your dot on it. With the 3D world on, the page keeps to the distance and height left, so it fits the numbers panel beside the road. Paged somewhere else, you get a line saying where the climb is instead, and a second sound when you reach the top. The road line at the top of a road ride now names the next climb ahead instead of the top of the one you are on.
