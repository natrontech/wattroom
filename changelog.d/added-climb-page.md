- On a road with a classified climb, your bike computer turns to a CLIMB page by itself as the climb begins, with a short sound: which climb of how many and its class, how far and how much height to the top, and the average gradient left, above a profile of the climb with your dot on it. Paged somewhere else, you get a line saying where the climb is instead, and a second sound when you reach the top. The road line at the top of a road workout now names the next climb ahead instead of the top of the one you are on.
