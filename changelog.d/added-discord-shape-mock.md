- The whole app is moving into one frame: a single sidebar holding your places, your rooms, and the places inside the room you are standing in — no more top nav, and no more tabs inside the room. Direct messages and friends stop being a floating drawer and a footnote on Home and become places of their own. Nine destinations become three — Rooms, Sessions, Progression, Ramp test and Sensors are absorbed by the pages they always belonged to. "Riding now" stops being a pink dot that read like an error and becomes the WattRoom mark's own equalizer bars, moving. The training view shows the whole crew — camera, watts, w/kg, rpm, bpm — and hands the screen over to the video when someone shares one. Switchable layouts (metrics-first / video-first / media-focus) retire; each of them becomes a place you can link to. TV mode is unchanged. Nothing has moved yet — this release carries the decision (ADR-0020) and a dev-only mock of every screen in the new shape.
