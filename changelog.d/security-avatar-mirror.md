- Your sign-in picture from Google, GitHub or Strava is now copied into WattRoom once and served by WattRoom. Until now your browser fetched that picture straight from the provider's CDN every time a room, roster or chat drew your face — which told them which room you were in and when. Nothing reaches a provider's servers any more, and the image policy that keeps a stranger's picture host out of the app is now enforced rather than only watched.
